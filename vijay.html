Jana Nayagan (transl. People's Hero) is an upcoming Indian Tamil-language political action thriller film[4] directed by H. Vinoth and produced by KVN Productions. The film stars Vijay, Pooja Hegde and Bobby Deol in the lead roles, alongside Mamitha Baiju, Gautham Vasudev Menon, Prakash Raj, Narain and Priyamani. It is intended to be the final film appearance of Vijay before his entry into politics, as well as KVN's first Tamil language production. The film was officially announced in September 2024 under the tentative title Thalapathy 69, as it is Vijay's 69th film as a lead actor, and the official title was announced in January 2025. Principal photography commenced the following month in Chennai continued by a schedule in Payanoor, which was again followed by two additional schedules also held at the former location, and wrapped by late June 2025. The film has music composed by Anirudh Ravichander, cinematography handled by Sathyan Sooryan and editing by Pradeep E. Ragav. Jana Nayagan is scheduled to be released on 9 January 2026 in theaters.
